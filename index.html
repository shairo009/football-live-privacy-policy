Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

I made Football Live Wallpaper as a simple, free live wallpaper app that anyone can enjoy. Before you use it, I want to be completely upfront about what the app does and does not do with your phone and your data.

What the App Is

Football Live Wallpaper puts animated 3D footballs on your home screen. The balls bounce around, react to your touch, and — if you turn it on — react to how you physically tilt your phone using the gyroscope. That's really all it does.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no form to fill out. The app does not ask for or access your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone.

Gyroscope / Motion Sensor

When you turn on the Gyro option in the app, Football Live reads your phone's accelerometer sensor to tilt the direction gravity pulls the balls. This reading happens only on your device, in real time, and is never saved to a file or sent anywhere. When the wallpaper is not visible, sensor reading stops completely.

Local Settings Storage

The app saves two small preferences on your device — whether Gyro is on or off, and the speed level you last chose — so it remembers your settings next time. This data lives only on your phone and is never backed up to any server or transmitted anywhere.

Internet Access

Football Live Wallpaper works completely offline. It does not connect to the internet, does not download anything after installation, and does not communicate with any server, ever.

Advertisements and Analytics

The app does not show ads of any kind. It does not include any advertising SDK, analytics library, crash reporter, or usage tracker. Nothing about how you use the app is recorded or sent anywhere.

Third-Party Services

No third-party services are embedded in this app. There are no integrations with Facebook, Google Analytics, Firebase, or any similar service.

Children

Football Live Wallpaper does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page before releasing that version. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect when the change was made.
