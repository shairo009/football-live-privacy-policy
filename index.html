Privacy Policy for Football Live

Effective date: April 20, 2026

Football Live is a simple 3D football wallpaper-style app. The app lets you watch footballs bounce inside a full-screen 3D space and, if you choose, use your phone's motion sensor to control the direction of gravity.

Information We Collect

Football Live does not collect personal information from users.

The app does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos, or files, and does not include any login system.

Motion Sensor / Gyro Use

Football Live can use your device's motion/orientation sensor when you turn on the Gyro feature. This is only used to move the footballs on your screen based on how you tilt your phone.

This motion data is processed on your device only. It is not stored, uploaded, shared, or used to identify you.

Internet, Ads, and Tracking

Football Live does not show ads and does not use advertising trackers.

Football Live does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

The app does not use analytics services, does not track users across apps or websites, and does not create a user profile.

Children

Football Live is a general entertainment app. It is not designed to collect information from children, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Data Security

Since Football Live does not collect personal information, there is no personal user database stored by the app. The gyro feature works locally on your phone.

Changes to This Policy

If the app changes in the future and starts using new features that affect privacy, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the developer email shown on the Football Live Google Play Store listing.
